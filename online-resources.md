# Online Resources

To reconstruct 2nd century cultural literature context of Roman satire in Early Christian texts,
it is useful to learn about online resources and tools that are available to the public.

- [Early Christian writings](http://www.earlychristianwritings.com/)

Here you can read translations of texts from Early Christianity.

- [Perseus Digital Library](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/)

Large collection of texts, including Roman and Greek literature.

- [Elpenor's Home of the Greek World](https://www.ellopos.net/elpenor/default.asp)

Many Greek texts, including Homer and the Septuagint and free lessons in Ancient Greek.

- [Topos Text](https://topostext.org/)

Explore places, texts and people in the ancient world.

- [Seshat: Global History Databank](https://seshat-db.com/)

The most current and comprehensive body of knowledge about human history.

- [The Leon Levy Dead Sea Scrolls Library](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/)

View digital copies of the dead sea scrolls.
